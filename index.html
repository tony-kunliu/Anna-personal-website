About me

Accounting graduate, former early-childhood educator, mother of two.

I am Anna Qu (Qu Yaping 曲亚平), a driving instructor based in Canberra. I hold a Master of Accounting from the Australian National University (2014–2016) and a Bachelor of International Trade and Economics from Yantai University (2010–2014). Accounting trained me to be precise and organised; my years as an early childhood educator at YWCA Canberra — where I also completed a Certificate in Early Childhood Education and Care (2018–2020) — taught me to put learners at ease and build skills one step at a time.

In 2021, I founded Anna's Driver Training Services and began teaching as a government-registered instructor with ACT Golden Driving School (堪培拉金牌驾校). Five years on, my work covers government driving-test preparation and learner logbook sign-off, in English and Mandarin. Most of my learners are complete beginners, or drivers who feel anxious behind the wheel.

My students often say the same thing: with Anna, there is no need to fear mistakes. I break every manoeuvre into clear steps — the reasoning first, then the technique — and I never raise my voice. Confidence is not innate; it is built lesson by lesson, until you can drive independently and safely.

I am also a mother of two young children. My husband, Tony (Liu Kun 刘坤), is a driving instructor, and together we run ACT Golden Driving School. Motherhood taught me what learners truly want from a lesson: to get home safely. I teach every student as I would my own family.

“Nervousness is natural. We proceed at your pace, step by step, until you are ready.”

An honour and a trust

Invited to present at the Chinese Embassy — two years in succession.

For two consecutive years, Tony and I were invited to the Chinese Embassy in Canberra to brief embassy staff on the key differences between driving in Australia and China: right of way, roundabout rules, parking regulations, and pedestrian priority. For staff newly posted to Australia, overlooking these differences can mean carrying unsafe habits onto local roads. We are grateful for the Embassy's trust, two years running.
